Privacy

Your Data Is Yours.

We never sell it. We never share it without your explicit request.

Last updated: June 24, 2026

01

Information we collect

When you contact us or submit a form, we collect the details you choose to share: your name, firm or practice, email, mobile number, and anything you write to us. If you enter the Revolt platform, we collect the practice profile you provide so we can show you relevant deal economics.

02

How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, to run the engagement you asked for, and to operate the Revolt platform. Aggregate, anonymized signals about how the market is moving may be used internally to improve our model. That aggregate data is never advisor-identifiable.

03

We do not sell your data

We do not sell advisor data to firms, custodians, or anyone else. We do not share your information with any party outside Revolt without your written request.

04

How long we keep it

We retain your practice profile only for as long as you are in active conversation with Revolt. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will, subject to any record-keeping obligations confirmed during compliance review.

05

Your choices

You can ask to see what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted. You can opt out of any non-essential contact. To make a request, reach the desk using the contact details below.

06

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: email desk@revoltfinancial.com.
