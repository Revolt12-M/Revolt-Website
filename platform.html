Self-service valuation

Why we built it

We Have the Data Because We Did the Deals.

Every other tool you have seen in this space was built by software people who then went looking for relationships. Ours was built the other way around. Eric ran 200 firm relationships and hundreds of closed transitions before a single line of code was written.

What we do with your data

Your Data Is Yours. We Never Sell It. We Never Share It Without Your Explicit Request.

Revolt stores your practice profile only for as long as you are in active conversation with Revolt. Aggregate, anonymized data: what segments of the market are looking, where, how often. That is used internally to improve the model. We do not sell that data to firms. We do not share it with anyone outside Revolt without your written request.
