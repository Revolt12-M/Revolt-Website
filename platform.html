What this is

We are not a platform. We are a firm with tools sharper than yours.

Most software companies trying to enter this space build a tool, then try to acquire the relationships. We did it the other way around. Twenty years of relationships. 190 firm contracts. Then a tool, built by the people who run the deals, to put that network on one screen.

The platform is the work, made tangible. It is not a replacement for your recruiter. It is what your recruiter brings to the first conversation.

247 advisors on the early-access list. In private beta with select Revolt prospects through 2026.
Three pillars

Self-service valuation. Full-network offer modeling. Practice intelligence.

Pillar 01

Self-service valuation

Enter your practice profile. The platform returns a valuation across three lenses, cash flow, strategic, succession, with the inputs visible. The same model we run on M&A deals.

Pillar 02

Full-network offer modeling

The 190-firm contract network fed directly into the model. Every offer priced to your specific practice, within 10 basis points. Updated quarterly.

Pillar 03

Practice intelligence

Once you have a baseline, the platform tracks the levers, production mix, fee model, client concentration, equity structure, that move your number. The same 27 levers Eric uses on every advisor call.

Why we built it

We built the data because we did the deals.

Every other platform you have seen in this space was built by software people who then went looking for relationships. Ours was built the other way around. Eric ran 190 firm relationships and hundreds of closed transitions before a single line of code was written. The dataset is not synthetic. It is the residue of work we have already done.

That is why the model is accurate within ten basis points. That is why the network is 190 deep, not five. That is why your recruiter can walk you through every line and tell you why it is what it is. The platform exists to put twenty years of work on one screen, in front of you, before you have to commit to anything.

What we do with your data

Your data is yours. We never sell it. We never share it without your explicit request.

The platform stores your practice profile only for as long as you are in conversation with a Revolt recruiter. Aggregate, anonymized data: what segments of the market are looking, where, how often. That is used internally to improve the model. We do not sell that data to firms. We do not share it with anyone outside Revolt without your written request.
