The operating principle

You will not find named testimonials on this website.

There are two reasons for that. The first is that the financial services industry runs on discretion. The advisors and firms we work with do not want their transitions turned into marketing content, and we are not willing to trade their privacy for our lead generation.

The second reason is simpler: when your name is the one on the deal, you will not want it on a website either. The same confidentiality that protects our past clients will protect you.

What we will show you is the work itself. Real deal structures (anonymized). Real economics. Real patterns from hundreds of transitions. The kind of intelligence most recruiters will not share because they do not have it. If you want names, there are firms that will give you names. We will give you a track record, a process, and a handshake. That is what we trade on.

Questions, answered

The eight that come up first.

How long does a move take?

Typically 90 days from intake to signed deal, but the timeline runs on your readiness, not ours. Some teams take 12 months. Some take 30 days.

What does Revolt actually cost me?

Nothing direct. You do not pay us out of pocket. We can walk you through the full structure on a call.

How do you avoid conflicts of interest across 200 firms?

We do not have favorites. No firm gets preferred placement. The 200-firm network exists so we never have to push a firm that does not fit you.

Can I talk to you without committing?

Yes. The first conversation is thirty minutes. No commitment. No follow-up unless you ask for it.

What if I am not ready to move for two years?

That is the right time to start the conversation. The advisors who get the best deals plan the move years out.

What size practice do you work with?

We work with advisors at all sizes of production. Every deal gets a senior consultant and a full team behind them.

What happens to my team and clients?

Your team can move with you. Your clients move with you. We map both before the offer goes out.

Will my current firm find out?

No. We have never publicized a team we have moved. Discretion is operational, not aspirational.
