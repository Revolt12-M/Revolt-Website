How we run a deal

Seven stages. Each one a discipline, not a checklist.

01 Engagement Day zero

Confidential intake. Scope, fees, deal team agreed.

02 Valuation Weeks 1–3

Three-lens model. Cash flow, strategic, succession. Each lens is its own analysis.

03 Positioning Weeks 3–5

Practice prepared for buyer view. Materials, financials, narrative.

04 Buyer curation Weeks 4–6

Strategic + financial buyer universe mapped. Curated, not blasted. If your name shows up in an industry newsletter, we failed at stage four.

05 Bidding Weeks 6–10

Structured competing bid process. Apples-to-apples terms, not just headline price.

06 Negotiation Weeks 10–16

Multi-round. Price, structure, earnouts, retention, regulatory close conditions.

07 Close & integration Weeks 16–28

Regulatory close. 90-day post-close integration support.

Valuation methodology

Three lenses. Whichever produces the highest defensible number wins.

Lens 01

Cash flow

What the practice earns, in the buyer’s hands.

Inputs

Revenue. EBITDA. Run-rate adjustments. Normalization. DCF.

Lens 02

Strategic

What the practice enables for the buyer.

Inputs

Geographic fit. Client segment fit. Capability gap. Cross-sell unlocked.

Lens 03

Succession

What the practice risks losing if the deal does not happen.

Inputs

Key-person concentration. Attrition risk. Retention dependency.
